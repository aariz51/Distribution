## 2. The product

**SafeChoice** — Know what is really inside any product you buy

Scan a product label and SafeChoice reads the ingredients for you: what each one is, why it matters, and whether it fits the way you eat.

- **Category:** health
- **Platforms:** ios, android
- **Audience:** Shoppers who read labels: parents, people managing allergies and intolerances, and anyone avoiding specific ingredients


Features, in the priority the founder set:

1. **Scan any product label**
2. **Know what is really inside**
3. **Ask your AI ingredient expert**
4. **Remember every product check**
5. **Learn to read labels better**
