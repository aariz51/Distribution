## Concept
'Read between the lines', told with no screenshots: only type, geometry and one mark. A question slams in from frame one. Its answer collapses into a single dot, the atom of the film, which is born, turns into a spinning diamond, speed-ramps into a razor of light and opens into the app icon: LabelWise. From there the film reads a label the way the app does: a machine line types itself, three glass cards explain every ingredient, a button is pressed and the camera dives through it into the four things it does. It closes where it began: a circle opening onto the mark.
